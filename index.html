    the door

    The ring is for contributors only. Anyone can hang out in the Discord, but a room on this map comes from contributing. If you want to contribute, email Axiss.

    Contributors get invited to the human-wad org on GitHub. Make your membership public on its People page, then open a pull request on the ring's repo that adds your room to members.json:

    { "name": "your-name", "gh": "your-github-username", "url": "https://your-site.example" }

    A check reads every pull request. It closes the ones from outside the org, and it only lets you add or change your own room.
